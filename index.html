ABOUT SWIFTS

One of nature's
greatest travellers.

Swifts are extraordinary birds. They spend much of their lives in the air, feeding, drinking and even sleeping while flying.

Every year they make an incredible journey between the UK and Africa. In spring, they return to the same towns and villages where they were born.

WHY SWIFTS NEED OUR HELP

Their ancient nesting places are disappearing.

For generations, swifts have returned to the same towns, streets and buildings each summer. But many of the small gaps they once used for nesting are being lost.

70%

decline in the UK Swift population between 1995 and 2024.

01

Homes are becoming sealed

Swifts often nest in small gaps beneath roof tiles, eaves and old buildings. Renovation and modern construction can accidentally remove these spaces.

02

Food and weather matter too

Swifts depend entirely on flying insects for food. Changes in weather and insect availability can make successful breeding and migration more difficult.

During very hot weather, nesting spaces beneath roofs can reach dangerous temperatures, putting chicks at risk.

03

Their world is changing

Swifts rely on flying insects for food and travel enormous distances each year. Changes in weather, habitat and food availability can affect them throughout their journey.

When a nesting entrance disappears, the returning swift may still come back looking for the home it remembers.

🏠 Lost nesting spaces

Swifts traditionally nest in small gaps under roof eaves and within older buildings. Modern building work can seal these entrances.

🌡️ Increasing heat

Hotter summers can make nesting spaces dangerously warm for young birds, adding another challenge.

🌍 A changing world

Swifts face challenges throughout their migration, including changes to weather, food supplies and habitat.

HOW YOU CAN HELP

Give swifts a better chance close to home.

Swifts live alongside us, so some of the most effective conservation work can happen on our own homes, streets and buildings.

01

Protect existing nests

If swifts already use a gap beneath your roof, eaves or tiles, try to keep that entrance available whenever building work is carried out.

02

Add a swift box

A purpose-built swift nest box can provide a new nesting opportunity where traditional spaces have disappeared.

See our handmade swift boxes →
03

Include swift bricks

Swift bricks can be built directly into new homes and extensions, providing permanent nesting spaces without changing the appearance of the building significantly.

04

Talk to your neighbours

A few suitable nesting places close together can make a street much more welcoming for returning swifts.

05

Speak to builders

Builders, roofers and developers can make a huge difference by protecting existing nest sites and including new ones in their work.

06

Spread the word

The more people who understand why swifts are disappearing, the more nesting spaces we can protect and create.
